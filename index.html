Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our IT support, repair, build, and website services.

Need Assistance?

Need help with a laptop, desktop, website, or custom build? We’re here to make the process simple and reliable.

Contact Support

Where do you offer support?

We support clients locally and remotely, depending on the service needed. We can also guide you through setup, troubleshooting, and hardware upgrades from a distance.

How do I book a service?

You can contact us through the contact form or direct message and we’ll help you choose the right service for your needs, timeline, and budget.

Do you provide custom recommendations?

Yes. We assess your goals and recommend the most suitable solution, whether that means a repair, upgrade, custom PC build, or website package.

Can you help with hardware repairs?

Absolutely. We help diagnose and fix common hardware issues, performance problems, and compatibility concerns to get your system working properly again.

What payment methods are available?

We can discuss payment options based on the type of service and your preference. We’ll make the process clear before work begins.

When will I receive updates on my service?

We keep clients informed throughout the process and provide updates at key milestones so you know exactly what’s happening with your project.

Can I request changes after booking?

Yes, changes can often be discussed depending on the stage of the project. We’ll do our best to accommodate your request while keeping the timeline on track.
